How contract driving with Precision works

Contractor roles only. Here's how the opportunity and the pay work.

The short version

Contractor, not employee

These are independent contractor opportunities. We're not hiring hourly or salaried employees through this page.

Paid for each piece delivered

Your pay is based on the pieces you deliver, not on hours worked.

You provide the tools

Your own suitable vehicle, insurance, required documents, fuel and tools.

How pay works

You're paid for each piece delivered. A fuel surcharge may apply depending on the contract or program, and when it does, it's paid out every two weeks.

Earnings are performance based. No two drivers are the same, and earnings are never forecasted or guaranteed. We don't promise specific hours or routes.

Questions drivers ask

Is this a job?

No. These are independent contractor opportunities, not employee positions.

Are there hourly or full-time employee positions?

No. We recruit contractors only. This page doesn't offer hourly or salaried employee positions.

How am I paid?

You're paid for each piece delivered. A fuel surcharge may apply depending on the contract or program. Earnings are performance based, and we don't forecast or promise specific earnings, hours or routes.

When is the fuel surcharge paid?

Where a fuel surcharge applies, it's paid out every two weeks. Whether one applies depends on the contract or program.

Do I need my own vehicle?

Yes. Contractors provide their own suitable vehicle, insurance, required documentation, fuel and tools. See which vehicles are accepted at each location on the vehicles page.
