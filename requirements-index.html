The short version

You

A full driver's licence, 3 demerit points or fewer and a background check from the last 90 days.

Your vehicle

A suitable minivan, cargo van or small cube truck.

Your paperwork

Your licence, driver's abstract, background check and a few more business documents.

Your business

You're an independent contractor. You provide your own vehicle, insurance, fuel and tools.

Driver requirements

  • A full driver's licence
  • No more than 3 demerit points on your driver's abstract
  • A criminal background check from the last 90 days that hasn't been pulled for another transportation company

Why these?

Safe, reliable drivers are the heart of every delivery. Every driver meets the same requirements, whatever their experience.

Vehicle requirements

Vehicles we recruit for

Minivans, cargo vans such as the Mercedes Sprinter or Ford Transit, and some small cube trucks.

Accepted vehicles can vary by location and contract. You'll see what applies to you when you apply.

Documents to gather

  • Your driver's licence
  • Your driver's abstract
  • A criminal background check from the last 90 days (one that hasn't been pulled for another transportation company)
  • A few more business documents

You run your own business, so you'll need business paperwork to match.

You run your own business

You're an independent contractor, not an employee. You provide your own suitable vehicle, insurance, required documents, fuel and tools.

You're paid for each piece delivered, and a fuel surcharge may apply depending on the contract or program.

Earnings are performance based. No two drivers are the same, and earnings are never forecasted or guaranteed.

Questions drivers ask

Is this a job?

No. These are independent contractor opportunities, not employee positions.

How am I paid?

You're paid for each piece delivered. A fuel surcharge may apply depending on the contract or program. Earnings are performance based, and we don't forecast or promise specific earnings, hours or routes.

Do all drivers meet the same requirements?

Yes. Every driver has to meet our onboarding requirements, including a full licence, no more than 3 demerit points and a background check from the last 90 days that hasn't been pulled for another transportation company.

Which vehicles can I use?

We recruit for minivans, cargo vans and some small cube trucks. Accepted vehicles vary by location.

Can I be paired with an existing team?

Yes. Check out our Driver Match Program and request a team.
