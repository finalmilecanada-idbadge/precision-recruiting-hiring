Questions drivers ask

Which vehicles can I use?

It depends on the location. Across our locations we currently recruit for cargo vans, minivans and SUVs. See the table above for your city.

Which locations accept cargo vans?

Cargo vans are accepted in London, Hamilton, Oakville, Pickering, Barrie, Orillia, Ottawa, Winnipeg, Montreal and Quebec City.

Can I use an SUV?

SUVs are accepted in Windsor, London, Barrie, Winnipeg, Montreal and Quebec City. They aren't currently recruited at the other locations in the table.

Who provides the vehicle, fuel and insurance?

You do. Contractors provide their own suitable vehicle, insurance, required documentation, fuel and the tools they need to deliver.
